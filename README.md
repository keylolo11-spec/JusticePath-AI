JusticePath AI

Academic & Career Support for Criminal Justice Students

JusticePath AI is an AI-powered web application designed to help college students studying Criminal Justice, Criminology, Psychology, and related fields. The application provides educational support with studying, research, writing, and career preparation.

Problem

Criminal justice students often have to balance coursework, research papers, exams, writing assignments, and career planning. It can be difficult to find quick and useful guidance in one place.

JusticePath AI addresses this problem by providing students with an AI-powered assistant that can answer questions and provide educational guidance.

Target Users

JusticePath AI is designed for:

* Criminal Justice students
* Criminology students
* Psychology students
* Students interested in criminal psychology
* Students working on criminal justice research
* College students looking for study and writing support
